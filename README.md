# TATA-project

Project: TATA Project by Forage - Power BI Analysis

Problem:
"An online retail store has hired you as a consultant to review their data and provide insights that would be valuable to the CEO and CMO of the business. The business has been performing well and the management wants to analyse what the major contributing factors are to the revenue so they can strategically plan for next year." (According to the Forage project)

What I Did:

The project involves cleaning and preparing the dataset, followed by creating various visualizations to highlight insights. Key performance indicators (KPIs), trends, and patterns are visualized through bar charts, line graphs, and tables. The data was manipulated using Power BI's powerful data modeling and transformation features, and several custom metrics were calculated to provide deeper insights into the business processes.

Tools Used:

Power BI and dataset provided by Forage

Recommendation:

Based on the insights gathered from the various charts, it is evident that while certain countries and customers contribute significantly to revenue, there are opportunities for strategic growth and optimization. The top-performing countries like the Netherlands, EIRE, and Germany show a strong correlation between revenue and quantity, suggesting that focusing on these regions with targeted marketing and enhanced supply chain strategies could drive further success. Additionally, the customer revenue data highlights a small group of key customers who generate a major portion of the revenue; nurturing these relationships through loyalty programs and personalized offerings could maximize customer lifetime value.

The monthly revenue trend for the 2011 shows fluctuations, particularly the sharp drop in December, which could be attributed to seasonal variations. Addressing this through seasonal promotions and stock management might help stabilize revenue. Lastly, expanding focus to underperforming regions and diversifying the customer base is crucial for sustained growth, ensuring that new markets are captured and current markets are better supported.

Conclusion:

The overall data provides valuable insights into revenue generation across regions, customer segments, and time periods. By focusing on high-performing markets, strengthening customer retention efforts, and addressing seasonal revenue fluctuations, the company can enhance its profitability. There is clear potential for growth through better market segmentation, targeted expansion into underperforming regions, and optimizing the relationship with top customers. Taking these strategic actions will lead to more consistent growth and a better-balanced revenue stream in the future.
